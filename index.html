About

What this tool does and how grades are calculated.

🎓 Student Grade Calculator

This dashboard helps you record CA, Assignment, and Exam scores for each student, then automatically works out their total, average, letter grade, and pass/fail status.

How grades are calculated

Total = CA + Assignment + Exam (each out of 100). Average = Total ÷ 3. The average then maps to a letter grade using the scale below, and any average of 50 or higher is marked as a pass.

A: 80 – 100
B: 70 – 79
C: 60 – 69
D: 50 – 59
E: 40 – 49
F: 0 – 39

Data storage

Student records are saved to your browser's local storage, so they'll still be here the next time you open this page on the same device and browser.
